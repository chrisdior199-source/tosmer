Privacy Policy

TosMer mobile application  |  Effective date: June 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vanna Vannak ("we", "us", or "our"), the operator of the TosMer mobile application (the "App"), collects, uses, shares, and protects your information. It also describes your rights and how to contact us. By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Data controller: Vanna Vannak, Ou Ambel, Serei Saophoan, Banteay Meanchey, Cambodia. Contact: support@tosmer.com.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information. When you sign in, we collect your phone number and/or a linked messaging account identifier (such as Telegram) used to create and access your account.
  • Activity information. Your watch history, favorites, search activity, and in-app rewards/points and check-in activity, used to provide and personalize the service.
  • Device and technical information. Device model, operating system version, app version, language, network type, app crash logs, and analytics/diagnostic identifiers, used to keep the App stable and secure.
  • Push notification token. If you enable notifications, we process a device messaging token so we can send you service notifications.

We do not collect payment or financial information, because the App does not currently offer in-app purchases. The App does not display third-party advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App and its content;
  • To remember your preferences, watch history, favorites, and rewards;
  • To diagnose problems, prevent fraud or abuse, and protect the security of the service;
  • To send service-related notices and, where you have enabled them, push notifications.

3. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable law (such as the EU/UK GDPR) requires a legal basis, we rely on: performance of our contract with you (to operate your account and deliver the service); our legitimate interests (to secure and improve the service); and your consent (for example, push notifications), which you may withdraw at any time.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for third-party advertising. We share information only:

  • with trusted service providers who process data on our behalf (see Section 5);
  • where required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the service;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

The App relies on a limited number of third-party providers to operate, including providers for account authentication, analytics and crash reporting, push notifications, and secure cloud storage and hosting. These providers process data only as needed to perform their services and are bound by their own privacy and security obligations.

6. International Data Transfers

Your information may be stored and processed on servers located outside your country of residence, including in jurisdictions whose data-protection laws may differ from your own. Where required, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information receives an adequate level of protection.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and to comply with our legal obligations. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for adult users (18+) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law, you may request to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@tosmer.com.

Account and data deletion. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time:
