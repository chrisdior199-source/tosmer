Delete Your TosMer Account

Operated by Vanna Vannak  |  App: TosMer (com.tosmer.app)

You can request deletion of your TosMer account and associated data at any time, either inside the App (Profile → Settings → Delete Account) or by submitting a request on this page. You do not need to reinstall the App to make a request.

How to request deletion

Send us a deletion request using the button below. Please send it from, or include, the phone number or account identifier linked to your TosMer account so we can locate it.

Request account deletion

Or email us directly at support@tosmer.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

What data is deleted

When your request is processed, we delete the personal data associated with your account, including:

  • your account identifier (phone number / linked messaging account);
  • your watch history, favorites, and search activity;
  • your in-app rewards/points and check-in records;
  • your push notification token.

What may be retained

We may retain a limited amount of information where required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or prevent fraud and abuse. Such data is kept only as long as necessary and is then deleted or anonymized.

Timeframe

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days. We may contact you to verify your identity before completing the request.
